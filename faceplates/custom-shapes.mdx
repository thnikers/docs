title: "Use Custom Faceplate Shapes in PlanMark"

---

PlanMark ships with a clean, neutral default faceplate shape that works well on most building plans. When your project requires branded documentation, hardware-specific graphics, or a faceplate style that matches your organisation's standards, you can replace the default shape with a custom image. Custom shapes apply to all callouts of the selected variant — swap in your image once and every matching callout on the plan updates automatically.

## Recommended Image Specifications

Before uploading, prepare your image to meet these specifications for the sharpest results:

| Property | Single-Port | Dual-Port |
|----------|-------------|-----------|
| **Format** | PNG (transparent background) or SVG | PNG (transparent background) or SVG |
| **Dimensions** | 200 × 200 px | 200 × 300 px |
| **Port label area** | One clearly defined empty region | Two clearly defined empty regions, stacked |
| **Background** | Transparent | Transparent |

Keep port label areas as open, uncluttered regions inside the shape so that PlanMark can overlay the label text cleanly without it competing with the graphic detail.

## Upload a Custom Shape

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Faceplate Shape Settings">
    Navigate to **Settings → Faceplate Shapes** in the PlanMark sidebar. This panel lists your currently active shapes for both the single-port and dual-port variants.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a New Custom Shape">
    Click **Add Custom Shape**. A dialog opens prompting you to name the shape and select the file to upload.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Your Image File">
    Select a PNG or SVG file from your computer. For best results, use a PNG with a transparent background or a clean SVG at the recommended dimensions. PlanMark accepts files up to 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Assign the Shape to a Variant">
    After the upload completes, use the **Apply to** dropdown to set the new shape as the active graphic for **Single-Port**, **Dual-Port**, or **Both**. You can maintain different custom shapes for each variant simultaneously.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Apply">
    Click **Save**. PlanMark immediately re-renders all existing callouts on the current plan using the new shape. No manual refresh or re-upload of your plan is required.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Define Port Label Placement With the Shape Editor

<Tip>
  After uploading your custom image, open it in the built-in shape editor (click the pencil icon next to your shape in the Faceplate Shapes panel). Use the editor to drag and resize the port label text zones — the areas where PlanMark overlays the Port 1 and Port 2 label text. Precise placement ensures your labels land exactly inside the port slot regions of your graphic, not on a border or decorative element.
</Tip>

## Reverting to the Default Shape

<Note>
  To restore the PlanMark default faceplate, go to **Settings → Faceplate Shapes**, select the variant you want to reset, and click **Revert to Default**. Your uploaded custom shapes are not deleted — they remain available in the shape library so you can reapply them at any time.
</Note>

## Shape Complexity and Plan Legibility

<Warning>
  Very complex or high-contrast custom shapes — for example, images with detailed textures, dark fills, or heavy borders — may obscure the underlying building plan details beneath the callout. Review your annotated plan at full zoom after applying a custom shape to confirm that critical plan elements (walls, dimensions, other symbols) remain visible. If legibility is affected, simplify the shape or reduce its opacity using the **Callout Opacity** slider in **Settings → Display**.
</Warning>
