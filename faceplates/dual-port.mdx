A dual-port faceplate callout appears on your building plan when PlanMark finds that a location row in your Excel spreadsheet has values in both the Port 1 and Port 2 columns. PlanMark renders a taller rectangular callout styled to resemble a 1-gang dual-port wall faceplate, with two clearly separated port slots stacked vertically inside the shape.

## Visual Layout

The callout is anchored to the detected symbol on the plan and contains three key elements:

- **Location number** — displayed prominently at the top of or adjacent to the callout, matching the row number in your spreadsheet.
- **Port 1 slot (top)** — the upper region inside the faceplate shape, showing the Port 1 label.
- **Port 2 slot (bottom)** — the lower region inside the faceplate shape, showing the Port 2 label.

This stacked layout mirrors the physical appearance of a real dual-port wall faceplate, making it intuitive to read directly on the plan without a separate legend.

## Port Slot Order Is Fixed

<Note>
  Port 1 is always rendered in the top slot and Port 2 is always rendered in the bottom slot. This order reflects standard faceplate conventions and cannot be swapped. If your installation uses a different physical ordering, update the port label values in your spreadsheet to match the order you want displayed.
</Note>

## Worked Example

Consider a spreadsheet row where:

| Location | Port 1 | Port 2 |
|----------|--------|--------|
| 1        | A-01   | A-02   |

PlanMark reads this row, sees that both Port 1 and Port 2 are filled, and places a dual-port faceplate callout at the position of symbol #1 on the plan. The callout shows the number **1**, with **A-01** in the top slot and **A-02** in the bottom slot. A technician can glance at the plan and know immediately that both ports at outlet 1 are patched — Port 1 to A-01 and Port 2 to A-02.

## Naming Conventions for Self-Describing Callouts

<Tip>
  Use a consistent port naming convention — such as patch panel ID followed by port number (for example, `PP2-A01`) — so that every dual-port callout is self-describing at a glance. When both slots follow the same format, reviewers can instantly understand the patch destination without cross-referencing the spreadsheet. Establish your naming convention before populating the spreadsheet to avoid relabeling later.
</Tip>
