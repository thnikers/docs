Welcome to PlanMark — the tool that takes the manual work out of annotating building plans. Upload a plan, tell PlanMark which symbols to look for, connect your Excel spreadsheet of port assignments, and PlanMark automatically detects every symbol instance, numbers each one, and overlays a faceplate callout annotation showing the correct port data. Whether you're a network installer, facilities manager, or AV integrator, PlanMark turns hours of hand-labelling into a single automated workflow.

## Workflow at a Glance

Get a feel for the full PlanMark process before you dive into the details.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Your Building Plan">
    Import a PDF or high-resolution image of your building plan into PlanMark. Multi-page plans are supported — each page is processed independently.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure Symbols to Detect">
    Provide one or more reference images of the symbol you want PlanMark to find — for example, a faceplate outlet icon. PlanMark uses these references to scan the plan for matching instances.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import Your Excel Spreadsheet">
    Upload the Excel workbook that contains your port assignment data. Each row corresponds to one symbol location and carries the port 1 and optional port 2 values that will appear in the callout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and Export the Annotated Plan">
    PlanMark numbers every detected symbol and overlays a faceplate callout at each location. Review the results in the interactive viewer, make any adjustments, then export the finished plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>
